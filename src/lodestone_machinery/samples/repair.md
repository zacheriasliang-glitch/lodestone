# Ceramic record maintenance

For a ceramic test record with a missing photograph, preserve the original entry and mark the photograph unavailable. Attach a replacement only when its tile identity is established. Keep the replacement event and original absence visible.

For a ceramic result that contradicts the prediction, retain both. Describe the observed difference before proposing the next change. A repaired record does not turn the failed firing into a successful one.

The example deliberately supplies instructions and observations as text. The machinery selects literal passages; deciding whether the procedure is sufficient requires a person or a separately supplied reasoner.
